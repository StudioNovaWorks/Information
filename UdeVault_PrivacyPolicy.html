Privacy Policy — UdeVault

Last Updated: November 18, 2026
Developer: Studio Nova Works

Studio Nova Works ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your data when using the Google Chrome extension "UdeVault - Full Course Transcript & Resource Archiver for Udemy" (the "Extension"). This Privacy Policy explains how the Extension handles information.

1. No Collection of Personal Data

The Extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, account credentials, or payment details), browsing history, or learning progress.

The Extension does not require user registration or account login.

2. Settings Stored Locally on Your Device

To remember your preferences across sessions, the Extension stores only the following user settings locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local and the extension's localStorage:

  • UI display language preference (uiLang / lang)
  • Theme preference (Light / Dark mode)
  • Preferred transcript language
  • Export option toggles (official audio priority, inclusion of empty lectures, inclusion of resource links, and ZIP bundling of attachments)

These settings remain strictly on your local device. They are never synced via chrome.storage.sync or transmitted to our servers or any third party.

3. Course Data Processing and Network Requests

When you use the Extension on an enrolled Udemy or Udemy Business course page, it performs the following actions:

  • Reads lecture metadata, article text, caption tracks (WebVTT), and supplementary resource metadata
  • Formats the content into a Markdown file (.md) or packages it into a ZIP archive (.zip)

All parsing, formatting, and ZIP packaging take place entirely locally inside your browser (on-device), and the resulting files are saved directly to your local downloads. No transcripts or course content are ever sent to Studio Nova Works or any external third-party server.

Solely for the purpose of retrieving subtitle tracks and supplementary attachments requested by you, your browser sends direct HTTPS requests to Udemy and its official content delivery domains (*.udemy.com, *.udemycdn.com, *.amazonaws.com, and *.cloudfront.net).

4. Chrome Extension Permissions

The Extension requests only the minimum permissions necessary to operate:

  • sidePanel: Displays the Extension's workspace alongside the Udemy course player.
  • storage: Saves the local user preferences described in Section 2 on your device.
  • tabs: Checks the active tab's URL to verify whether it is a valid Udemy or Udemy Business course page (https://*.udemy.com/course/*) and updates the side panel state accordingly. Browsing history on other websites is never read or stored.
  • scripting: Applies the Extension's content script to already-open Udemy course tabs immediately after installation or update so you can use it without reloading the page.
  • Host Permissions (*.udemy.com, *.udemycdn.com, *.amazonaws.com, *.cloudfront.net): Required to read course curriculum data and download signed WebVTT caption tracks and lecture attachments directly to your device.

5. Third-Party Services, Analytics, and Advertising

  • The Extension does not use any analytics tools (such as Google Analytics), tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.
  • The Extension does not load or execute remotely hosted code.
  • We never sell, rent, share, or disclose user data to third parties.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

All locally stored preferences are permanently deleted when you uninstall the Extension from Chrome or clear your browser's extension storage/site data. Because no data is stored on any external server, no separate data deletion request is needed.

7. Compliance with Chrome Web Store Policies

The Extension's use of browser APIs and information adheres to the Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies, including the Limited Use requirements. Information accessed by the Extension is used exclusively to provide its core functionality and is never used for creditworthiness, advertising, or data brokering.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Extension's features, applicable laws, or Chrome Web Store requirements. Any updates will be indicated by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
